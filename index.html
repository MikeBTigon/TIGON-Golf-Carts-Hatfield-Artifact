Why buy from a preowned golf cart dealership?

According to TIGON, buying preowned from the Hatfield store rather than a private seller comes with dealership backing:1

Documented inspection

A multi-point technician inspection before listing, covering a battery load test on electric models, drivetrain, brakes, steering and electrical. The report is available at purchase.

Written condition disclosure

Cosmetic and mechanical condition is rated and disclosed in writing before you buy.

Certified pre-owned option

Confirmed CPO carts include a 60-day powertrain warranty; ask the store to verify an individual cart's status and terms.8

Financing for all credit

Lending partners cover every credit profile, with terms up to 84 months.

Trade-ins accepted

Your current cart's value can go toward your next one, including as a down payment.

Service after the sale

Factory-trained technicians at 15 TIGON service centers across 10 states.11
